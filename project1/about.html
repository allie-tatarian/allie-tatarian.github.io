About Us

The Lorem Ipsums are the premire wedding cover band of the south-eastern New England region! Our band members, Carly, Devin, and brothers Brad and John, have been playing together for over 5 years. They have played over 100 weddings throughout Rhode Island and Southeastern Massachusetts. Their style is playful, fun, and a little romantic. They love to lead a crowd in a line-dance, too!
